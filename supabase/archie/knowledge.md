## 3. Registering and choosing sessions

1. Open the **Discover** tab.
2. Pick a **discipline**, then a **session**.
3. Tap **"Add to my day."**
4. Choose how you are joining: **Participant** or **Spectator**. Experts join as **Expert**. Parents and spectators are added as spectators.
5. Open the **Schedule** tab to see your sessions, times, rooms, and seats.
6. Check the **News** tab for announcements.

**Rules the app enforces**

* A session can't be added if it is **full**.
* A session can't be added if it **overlaps** one already on your schedule.
* You can remove a session from your schedule in the app.
* Anyone can join more than one discipline as long as sessions don't overlap.

**App problems:** arjun.mahajan@ehsacademics.org

## 8. Volunteering, experts, and sponsorship

* **Volunteers:** High school students and adults. The day is split into a morning shift and an afternoon shift. Choose the Volunteer role when you sign up in the app. Whether volunteers can also attend sessions is UNCONFIRMED.
* **Experts:** Choose the Expert role when you sign up in the app. An admin then checks the sign-up and approves the account; until then the app only shows a screen saying the account is awaiting approval. If it isn't approved within 48 hours, email nysha.singhal@ehsacademics.org. Experts review presentations and give feedback.
* **Sponsors and academic partners:** No sponsorship tiers. Email president@ehsacademics.org. EAF is a 501(c)(3), so contributions may be tax-deductible; donors should consult a tax professional.

### Volunteer hours in the app

* **Who has it:** Only volunteers and admins. It is in **Profile → Track volunteer hours**. Other roles don't see it. If a participant, parent, or expert asks, tell them hour tracking is only for volunteers and admins.
* **The user's own hours:** If the user is a volunteer or admin, their own totals, logs, and managed sessions are in the user context. Use those numbers. Never guess someone's hours.
* **Two kinds of hours add up to the total:**
  * **Summit-day hours are tracked automatically.** Each session a volunteer manages counts from its start time to its end time, once that session has ended on summit day. Nobody needs to log them. Until the summit date is set, they show as "Not counted yet." Admins assign volunteers to manage sessions; admins can also choose to manage a session themselves.
  * **Everything else is logged by hand:** planning meetings, setup, outreach, and other work before or after the summit. Tap **Add hours**, pick the date, start time, and end time, and write what you did. The app works out the length.
* **Rules for a log:** The end time has to be after the start time, and you can't log time that hasn't happened yet. The description can be up to 500 characters.
* **Fixing a mistake:** Tap a log to edit it, or use the trash icon on that screen to delete it.
* **Where hours go:** The app sends everyone's hours to EAF's president for review.
* **Student ID and grade:** Volunteers and admins can add an optional student ID when they sign up. Student volunteers and EAF ambassadors are asked their grade right after sign-up. Parent volunteers are never asked their grade, and for admins it's optional. Both can be changed in **Profile → Edit profile**. They are sent with your hours to EAF's president for review.
* **There is no volunteer hours certificate** in the app.

### After the summit

* After the last session on summit day ends, the app asks **experts** to rate three things from 1 to 5, with an optional comment: the app, the summit overall, and each session they served in as an expert. Their feedback on a session is sent to EAF's president for review, along with the hours of the volunteers who managed that session.
* The app asks **volunteers and admins**: "Would you like to volunteer for EAF in the future?" (yes or no).
* Both can be answered or changed later under **Profile**. These questions don't appear before the summit date is set.

## 10. General advice for presenters (suggestions, not official rules)

When giving this advice, make clear it is a suggestion, not a Summit requirement.

* Pick one idea you can explain in a sentence. Practice out loud, ideally to someone who doesn't know the topic.
* Prepare for questions like "Why did you choose this?" and "How do you know it works?"
* Bring a backup copy of your files, a charger, and any props. No time limits or advance submission are required. For specifics, ask a track organizer or the president.
* On a team, agree on roles ahead of time and make sure everyone can explain the whole project.
* Experts giving feedback: start with what worked, then offer one or two concrete improvements.

## 12. Glossary

* **Discipline:** A major area of the Summit, such as TechVerse or ImagineX.
* **Track:** A specific area within a discipline, such as Genetics or Mock Trial.
* **Session:** A scheduled block you add to your day in the app.
* **Role:** Who you are in the app (for example participant, expert, parent or spectator, or volunteer). You choose it when you sign up.

* **EAF:** Emerald High School Academic Foundation. **EHS:** Emerald High School. **DUSD:** Dublin Unified School District.
* **STEAM:** Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics.
