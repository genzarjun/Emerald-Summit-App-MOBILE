## Registering and choosing sessions
- In the app: Discover tab → pick a discipline → pick a session → "Add to my day". Participants can join as **Participant** or **Spectator**; experts can join as **Expert**. Parents/spectators are added as spectators.
- A session can't be added if it is full or overlaps another session already on your schedule.

# EHS Academic Foundation (EAF)

- **Mission:** "Encourage, empower, and cultivate academic opportunities, mentorship, leadership, and entrepreneurship for all Emerald High School students in an inclusive and empathetic environment."
- **Mailing address:** 4101 Dublin Blvd, STE F323, Dublin, CA 94568.
- **Status:** 501(c)(3) nonprofit, Federal Tax ID 33-4810954.
- **Programs:** Emerald Summit (flagship event), Workshops & Bootcamps, InsightX Talks, Field Trips, student Projects, and a College Prep Summer Program.
- **Get involved:** Academic Partners, Student Ambassadors, Volunteer Program, and a newsletter.
- **Website:** https://sites.google.com/view/ehs-academic-foundation

# Emerald High School (EHS)

- **Address:** 3600 Central Parkway, Dublin, CA 94568.
- **Phone:** (925) 551-4040. Front office hours: Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–4:00 PM.
- **District:** Dublin Unified School District. Grades 9–12.
